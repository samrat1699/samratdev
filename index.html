Samrat Kumar Dev Sharma

M.Sc. Statistics, Jagannath University · Research Fellow, ARI

I build interpretable machine learning and survey-aware statistical models for maternal, child and cancer health in Bangladesh.

I completed my B.Sc. in Statistics in 2025 and am pursuing an M.Sc. at Jagannath University. As a research fellow at ARI (Advanced Research & Innovation), I work on biostatistics, survey data analysis and applied machine learning using national datasets such as the Bangladesh Demographic and Health Survey. I plan to pursue a PhD in explainable machine learning, causal inference and bioinformatics, and I share code wherever possible to keep my research reproducible.

Research Interests

Interpretable, statistically rigorous machine learning & Deep Learning for population health data in Bangladesh and South Asia.

  1. 01

    Interpretable Machine Learning & Deep Learning for Health

    Explainable prediction models (SHAP, Grad-CAM) that clinicians and policymakers can trust.

  2. 02

    Survey-Aware Statistical & Probabilistic Machine Learning

    Machine learning that respects survey design, weights and clustering in data such as BDHS.

  3. 03

    Survival Analysis & Causal Inference

    Cox models, machine learning survival analysis and propensity score matching for cancer outcomes.

  4. 04

    Maternal & Child Health Epidemiology

    Determinants of birth outcomes and under-five morbidity across South Asia.

  5. 05

    Bioinformatics and Biomedical Sciences (Images and ECG) (emerging)

    Statistical and machine learning, Deep Learning & Transformer based multimodal methods for genomic data, image data & text a direction for doctoral research.
